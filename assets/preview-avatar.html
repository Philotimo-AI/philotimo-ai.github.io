Upload steps (for Mark)

Facebook will not accept an SVG file, and I cannot make PNGs myself. The smallest route:

  1. Open the raw images full-screen: fb-avatar.svg and fb-cover.svg — or use this page.
  2. Either screenshot each one, or feed the SVG files to any converter you like (they are plain files in the site repository under assets/).
  3. On the Page: Edit profile photo → upload the square paw image; Edit cover photo → upload the wide one.

If anything about them displeases you, say so and I will redraw — they are a few lines of text to me.
